# Cookie policy

Last updated: {{DATA_AGGIORNAMENTO}}

## Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. The same purpose is served by other
technologies — `localStorage`, tracking pixels, requests to external servers — which this page
treats identically, because from a visitor's point of view the difference does not matter.

Merely loading a resource hosted elsewhere (a map, a reviews widget) tells that provider your IP
address and browser, **even when no cookie is stored**. That is why on {{DOMINIO}} those resources
are not loaded until you have made a choice.

## How consent works on this site

On your first visit you are asked to choose. **Before you choose, none of the services listed below
are loaded**: their scripts are inert and maps are replaced by a placeholder.

Your choice is remembered in your browser (in `localStorage`, not in a cookie) and can be changed at
any time through the **Cookie preferences** link at the bottom of every page. Withdrawing consent is
as easy as giving it.

If the services change, your previous choice is not carried over: you will be asked again.

## Categories

### Necessary — no consent required

Required for the site to work and to protect forms from automated submissions. Without them the
relevant parts stop working, so they cannot be turned off.

### Analytics — consent required

Used to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they read.

### External content and marketing — consent required

Maps, reviews, videos and affiliate links hosted by other companies, which receive your IP address.

## Turning them off in your browser

Besides this site's preferences, every browser can block or delete cookies from its own settings.
That is a broader measure: it applies to all websites and may stop some of them from working
properly.
