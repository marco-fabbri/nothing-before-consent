## What data is processed

### Data collected automatically during your visit

The servers hosting this site, and the providers of the services listed below, record technical data
that is unavoidable in any internet communication: IP address, browser and operating system, page
requested, date and time, referring page.

This data is used to deliver pages and to protect the site from abuse. It is not used to identify
visitors, nor combined with other data.

**Legal basis**: legitimate interest in providing and protecting the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Data you provide

<!-- Keep this section only on sites that have a contact form. -->

If you use the contact form you provide your name, surname, email address, phone number and the text
of your message. That data is used only to reply, and is delivered to the controller by email.

**Legal basis**: responding to your request, i.e. steps taken at your request prior to entering into
a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). This is not based on consent: you wrote asking for a reply, and the
reply needs that data.

The message field is free text: please **do not include health information or other special category
data** beyond what is needed for us to get back to you.

### Data collected by third-party services

This site embeds services hosted by other providers. They are not loaded until you have explicitly
chosen, as described in the [cookie policy]({{URL_COOKIE_POLICY}}), where each one is listed.

**Legal basis**: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time without affecting
the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

## Who receives it

Data is never sold or handed over. It is accessible to the technical providers that make the site
work — hosting, email delivery, and the services listed in the cookie policy — each only for the
part that concerns them.

Some providers may process data **outside the European Economic Area**. Where that happens, the
transfer relies on the safeguards in Chapter V GDPR, typically the standard contractual clauses
adopted by the European Commission.

## How long it is kept

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Server technical logs | as long as needed for service security, typically a few days |
| Messages sent through the form | as long as needed to handle the request and its consequences |
| Your cookie choice | until withdrawn, or until the services change |

## Your rights

You can ask for **access** to your data, **rectification**, **erasure**, **restriction** of
processing, **portability**, and you can **object** to processing based on legitimate interest.
Where processing is based on consent, you can **withdraw** it at any time — for third-party
services, through the *Cookie preferences* link at the bottom of every page.

Send requests to **{{EMAIL_CONTATTO}}**; they are answered within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a **complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority**
(www.garanteprivacy.it), or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence, if you
believe the processing infringes the law.

## Changes

If this notice changes substantially, the date at the top is updated and — when the change concerns
third-party services — consent is asked again rather than carried over.
